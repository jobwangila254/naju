About Naju Poultry

Our story, mission, and commitment to quality

Our Story

Naju Poultry began as a small family farm in Kwanza sub-location with a simple mission: to provide fresh, healthy, and affordable poultry products to our local community. What started with just a few chickens has grown into a trusted name in poultry farming, serving thousands of satisfied customers across Kitale, Eldoret, Nakuru, Nairobi, and surrounding regions.

Founded in 2018, we've spent over 8 years perfecting our farming practices, investing in modern technology while maintaining traditional farming values. Our commitment to quality has never wavered, and today we're proud to be one of the leading suppliers of fresh eggs, healthy chicks, and premium chicken products in the region.

Every product that leaves our farm carries our promise of freshness, quality, and care. We believe that good food starts with good farming, and that's exactly what we deliver to your table.
