Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do you deliver to my area?

We currently deliver to Lagos, Ibadan, Abeokuta, and surrounding areas. Contact us to confirm delivery to your specific location.

How fresh are your products?

All our products are farm-fresh. Eggs are collected daily, and chickens are processed to order to ensure maximum freshness.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, bank transfers, and mobile payments. Payment details are provided when you place your order.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes! We welcome farm visits during business hours. Please call ahead to schedule your visit and ensure someone is available to show you around.
